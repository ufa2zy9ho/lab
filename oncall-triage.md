# On-Call Triage Notes

Quick reference for incident response. Last updated: 2026-08-25.

- **Acknowledge** the alert within 5 minutes.
- **Assess** severity and impact using the dashboard.
- **Communicate** in the #incident Slack channel.
- **Act** based on runbook: restart service, rollback deploy, or escalate.
- **Document** timeline and actions in the postmortem template.

Checklist:
- [ ] Alert acknowledged?
- [ ] Runbook followed?
- [ ] Status updated?
